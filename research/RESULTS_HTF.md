# HTF BTCUSDT Experiment Results

## 1. Data range and source
Binance USDT-M public BTCUSDT perpetual klines from 2024-01-01 through the latest completed candle, capped at 2026-09-08 for deterministic reruns. Data is cached in data/htf. Funding uses a labeled -0.01% placeholder per 00:00/08:00/16:00 UTC crossing.

## 2. What changed vs the killed 5m spec
The killed adaptive 5m dual-engine system was not retuned. These are separate frozen higher-timeframe single-engine trend-pullback experiments: B1 trades 15m with completed 1h EMA21 bias, and B2 trades 1h with completed 4h EMA21 bias. No new indicators were added.

## 3. Full-sample trade counts (circuit breaker)
- B1 full sample Cost A next-open trades: 17 / cap 800
- B2 full sample Cost A next-open trades: 5 / cap 250
